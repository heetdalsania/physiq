Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Welcome to PhysiQ Engine. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Storage

PhysiQ Engine is designed with a local-first approach. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not store your data on any cloud servers. All of your data—including your profile, intake history, meals, and workout logs—is stored entirely locally on your device.

2. Camera Usage

Our app requests camera access strictly for the purpose of scanning food barcodes. The camera is used locally to read the barcode, and images are never stored, transmitted, or shared with us or any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the Open Food Facts API to retrieve nutritional information when you search for foods or scan a barcode. Only the search queries (such as the barcode or the food name) are sent to these services to fetch the relevant data. No personally identifiable information or user data is transmitted to them.

4. Analytics and Tracking

We respect your privacy and do not include any analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party SDKs that monitor your usage or collect personal data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our support channels.
